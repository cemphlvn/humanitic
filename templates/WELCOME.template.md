## 1 · Your `.local` — the sovereign vault
`.local` is **yours**, on your machine only. It is **hard-gitignored, unpublishable, never committed** —
**Article 0, the eternity clause**: a rule that cannot be removed, hidden, or even *proposed* away.
It holds your `_vector.yaml`, `machine.yaml`, secrets (encrypted at rest — Keychain/Secure Enclave),
`vault/` (your strategy IP), `theses/`, `calibration/`, `index.local/`. Your data never leaves.

## 2 · Humanitik — the open commons
An ecosystem where **everything is a node** — individuals, for-profit, non-profit organizations.
**Forever open · vector-tracked attribution · rewarded by contribution degree.** The **macro-kernel** is
the substrate node: engineer, prove, and (paper-)trade macro strategies. Use it, extend it, or build
your own node beside it.

## 3 · The boundary
```
PRIVATE (.local) ──────────────⟂────────────── PUBLIC (Humanitik)
your strategies · theses · keys                 de-identified lessons · failure autopsies
        stays with you, forever                 conceptual trigrams — attributed 95% you / 5% foundation
```
You contribute a *lesson*, not your edge. A strategy becomes a **trigram** — a public coordinate that
can't be reverse-engineered. The commons gets wiser; your secret stays secret; your name gets the credit.

## 4 · The ethos
Honesty is the metric (a refuted strategy is a result). You are sovereign (no live capital, no mainnet
trade, no `.local` published — without you). Every outward edge is gated and built to fail safe.
