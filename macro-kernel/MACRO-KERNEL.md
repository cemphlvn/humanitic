# Humanitic Macro-Kernel

> An honest, sovereign macro-strategy substrate that develops its own curiosity.

---

## Philosophy

The macro-kernel is not a trading bot. It:

1. **Refutes** — A refuted strategy is a *result*, not a defeat. The evidence court kills what doesn't
   hold edge out-of-sample; the failure database keeps the autopsy. Honesty over a lucky window.
2. **Measures in bits** — Edge is held *information* (bits) against a capacity wall `C(D)`; the court
   never claims past the wall. Bits measure what's known; Sharpe measures whether it was monetized.
3. **Respects measured limits** — The regime substrate inherits the lineage's walls (TC⁰ ceiling,
   abelian-only, the `bundle` capacity envelope, the KAM chaos wall) and *reports* them, never hides them.
4. **Stays sovereign** — Strategies live in `.local` under the eternity clause (Article 0); only
   de-identified lessons leave, and only through the publish guard, attributed under HUMANITIK.
5. **Earns its place** — As a node in the Humanitic graph it earns (contribution), pays (x402), and
   trades (CDP onchain) — every outward edge gated (budget, testnet, live token, drawdown KILL).

## The node in the graph

Everything in Humanitic is a node — **individuals, for-profit, and non-profit organizations**. The
macro-kernel is the **substrate node**: where a person or org engineers, proves, and (paper-)trades
macro strategies, contributes de-identified lessons to the commons, and is rewarded by contribution
degree.

The macro strategy, in three layers: **GLM-style agentic engineering** is the builder/operator;
**HD/VSA + JEPA** is the latent regime layer; **FinRL-X** is the execution-consistent finance layer;
**x402 + CDP** is the agent economy. We do not ask the model to predict prices — we ask it to engineer,
test, repair, and audit strategies inside a deployment-consistent architecture.
