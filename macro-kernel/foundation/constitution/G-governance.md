**Status: PROPOSED** · EDR (amendable in the forum; see `FORUM.md`). *Not Article 0.*

This EDR proposes the amendment process itself: the constitution (minus Article 0) is changed only
through **Ethics Decision Records** deliberated in the forum (`FORUM.md`). The loop is the same one
the whole system runs — propose → deliberate → the strongest objection survives → temperature-check
poll → a maintainer ratifies the node and credits contributors via HUMANITIK. The poll is advisory;
it informs the maintainer but never overrides a surviving objection. A refuted proposal is filed as a
result, not discarded silently — governance inherits the refutation ethos from the labnotes.

This EDR also fixes the **jurisdictional boundary**: governance may reshape how we work, what we
measure, and how we credit each other, but it has **no power over Article 0**. The eternity clause is
non-amendable, non-proposable, and non-justiciable; threads proposing to weaken `.local`
unpublishability are auto-closed by forum CI (`reject_article0_proposals`). Governance cannot vote
itself the authority to open the lock — that limit is what keeps a self-revising system trustworthy.
Everything else here is open to the strongest objection; ratification credits contributors.
