**Status: PROPOSED** · EDR (amendable in the forum; see `FORUM.md`). *Not Article 0.*

This EDR proposes that the commons be **attributed by construction**. HUMANITIK — the local knowledge
index, failure database, and attribution ledger — is the moat precisely because it remembers *who
learned what*. Every de-identified lesson, every filed autopsy of a strategy that died in the
evidence court, counts as a contribution and is credited to its author when the corresponding node or
EDR is ratified. A refuted idea is a result, not a defeat: the failure database is where most of the
value accrues, so the people who produce refutations are first-class contributors, not also-rans.

This EDR also commits the project to **trilingual access** as a social, not cosmetic, choice. Every
constitutional and architectural node leads with Turkish (the bridge between English and Chinese),
then English, then Chinese, so the commons is reachable across the three bootstrap languages. The
hard boundary remains: contributions are **de-identified** before they enter the commons — `.local`
never crosses the line, enforced by `foundation/privacy/publish_guard.py`, not by trust. Refinements
and the strongest objections are deliberated in the forum; ratification credits contributors.
