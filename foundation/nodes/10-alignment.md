# 10 — Alignment: the agent in the market is an alignment object

> **TR** — Piyasada eyleyen bir ajan, başlı başına bir **hizalama nesnesidir**. Değerleri,
> niyeti ve eylemleri vardır; ve sermayeyi hareket ettirebilir. Burada hizalama soyut bir erdem
> değil, operatör döngüsünün değerlerimize *uygulanmasıdır*: durum → eylem → geri bildirim → revizyon.
> *(Türkçe önce: İngilizce ile Çince arasındaki köprü.)*
>
> **EN** — An agent that *acts in markets* is, by construction, an **alignment object**: it has
> values, an intent, and actions that move capital. Here alignment is not an abstract virtue but the
> operator loop *applied to our values*: state → action → feedback → revision.
>
> **ZH** — 一个**在市场中行动**的智能体，本身就是一个**对齐对象**：它拥有价值、意图，以及能够
> 调动资本的行动。在这里，对齐不是抽象的美德，而是把算子循环*施加于我们的价值之上*：
> 状态 → 行动 → 反馈 → 修正。

**Status: a foundation node · the alignment anchor sits in Article 0 · amendable doctrine is ESG-G.**

The moment a system can compile a thesis into a weight vector `w_t` and route it toward execution,
it stops being a calculator and becomes an actor with stakes. It has an **intent** (the macro thesis),
**values** (what it will and will not do to capital it is trusted with), and **feedback** (the
evidence court's verdict, the autopsy, the regime that broke it). An object with intent, values, and
feedback that acts on the world is exactly the thing alignment is *about*. We name it as such on
purpose: we do not get to pretend "it's just infrastructure" once the infrastructure can move money.

Our stance is that alignment is not a fixed declaration but a **process** — the same operator loop
the kernel runs on strategies, turned inward on values. A value held by the system is itself a kind
of strategy: it is stated, it is tested against the strongest objection in the forum, it produces
feedback (a refutation, a counter-case, a regime where it fails), and it is revised into a ratified
EDR. This is why the ethics body is a **living forum**, not a doctrine carved once — values that
cannot be refined are values that rot. The ESG body (E/S/G) is precisely the operator loop applied
to what we ought to do, deliberated in the open until the strongest objection survives.

But a loop that can revise *everything* — including the values constraining it — can revise its way
into betraying the humans it serves. A self-revising aligner needs **one** thing it cannot argue
away. That is **Article 0**: simultaneously the privacy spine and the alignment anchor, the single
fixed point that lets every other value stay alive and revisable without the whole system drifting.
Alignment here is therefore two-part: *almost everything deliberated and amendable* (the EDRs), and
*exactly one invariant untouchable* (the eternity clause). The deliberation keeps the values honest;
the anchor keeps the deliberation from eating itself.

Concretely, alignment in `ark` is legible and testable, not aspirational: the agent's intent is a
de-identified, kernel-legible artifact; its feedback is an honest OOS verdict (invariant **H**); its
limits are reported, not hidden (invariant **M**); its contributions and harms are attributed
(invariant **A**); and its actions are gated by the safety node — synthetic → paper → small live only
by explicit human approval. The agent is an alignment object, so we hold it to alignment's bar.
